# 01: Remove Declined Times

Status: resolved

## What the owner said

The owner doesn't remember Declined Times and thinks it's a feature that should probably go. Not to be done during the Task screen simplification: pick it up afterwards.

## Decided when this was picked up

- Deleting a Task's time in Google places the Task again wherever it fits, which may be the same time.
- The Declined Times already saved are deleted with the feature.

## Where it reaches

- The word in `GLOSSARY.md`, and `.scratch/phase-2/spec.md` for why it was added.
- Google sync, Recalculation, the Task commands and the Declined Times list on the Task screen.

## Answer

Declined Times are gone: the list on the Task screen, the count on a Task's row, the word in the glossary and the saved table. See "Decided when this was picked up" for what deleting a time in Google does now.
